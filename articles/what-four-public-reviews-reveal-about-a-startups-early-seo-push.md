<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #ffffff; margin-bottom: 16px;">What Four Public Reviews Reveal About a Startup's Early SEO Push</h1>
<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/3a7583b341e8d3b26f43f522c62eb707.jpg" alt="What Four Public Reviews Reveal About a Startup's Early SEO Push" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 20px 0;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">It's rare to get a transparent, cross-platform look at how an early-stage product's backlink campaign actually played out — but that's essentially what happened when the team behind an AI research tool for prediction markets shared feedback about their directory submission process across several independent platforms.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">The consistent thread across all of it: the product started with a Domain Rating of zero, the submission workload was described as time-consuming to do manually, and the process of getting listed across a curated set of directories was described as efficient once it was handled in a structured way rather than piecemeal.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Within about a month, multiple public comments independently reported the same rough milestone — a Domain Rating climbing into the high teens, alongside the first meaningful referral traffic showing up from directory pages. Minor differences between the numbers reported on different platforms are easily explained by the fact that DR is recalculated as a third-party index updates, and the comments weren't posted at the exact same moment.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">A more recent, independently verified check placed the domain even further along, into the low twenties. The full set of public comments, along with the specific directories that produced live, checkable listings, is compiled in <a href="https://listingbott.com/blog/glypse-from-dr-0-to-21-with-a-gradual-directory-campaign/" style="color: #4da6ff; text-decoration: underline;">this case study on a gradual directory campaign</a>.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">What's valuable here isn't any single testimonial — it's the pattern across independent sources telling a consistent story, which is a far more reliable signal than any one glowing review.</p>
