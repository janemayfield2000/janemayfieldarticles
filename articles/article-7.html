What Software Buyers Actually Look For on Comparison Directories

Getting listed on a software comparison directory is only half the job — the other half is understanding what buyers on those platforms are actually looking for, because it's a very different mindset from someone browsing a general business directory.

Buyers on platforms like Capterra or Software Advice are usually already deep into a decision process. They're comparing feature sets, reading category-specific reviews, and filtering by use case, which means a vague, generic listing description gets skipped in seconds.

Category selection matters enormously here. Misclassifying your product — even slightly — kills discovery, because buyers filter by category before they ever see a product name. A listing in the wrong category is close to invisible, no matter how strong the copy is.

Commercial intent is the other variable worth optimizing for. These platforms attract people closer to a purchase decision than a general directory would, so the listing copy should speak to that stage — concrete use cases and differentiators, not broad brand messaging.

Getting this right across dozens of comparison platforms at once is exactly the kind of detail work a structured campaign handles better than an ad hoc submission.
